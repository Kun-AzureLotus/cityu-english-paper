## Turnitin interpretation

AI-writing percentage and similarity are independent metrics. Neither is automatically a misconduct verdict. The vendor acknowledges possible false positives and warns against using the AI result alone for adverse action.
https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/22774058814093-Using-the-AI-Writing-Report

Do not invent a CityU “safe AI threshold”, assume all lecturers share a cutoff, or treat the vendor's low-score display conventions as course permission. Do not diagnose hidden model features from punctuation or a style score.

## When permission is unclear

Request only the relevant course/assessment excerpt once. Identify the intended action: diagnostic feedback, grammar correction, substantive rewriting, translation, or new drafting. Record known limits. Continue work that does not depend on the missing answer, without asserting the resulting text is approved for submission.

## Report questions

Ask for the report if the task depends on particular highlights, not merely on writing quality. Inspect its title, date, metric and passages; do not infer the report from a percentage alone. You may help explain the student's actual writing process and organize pre-existing supporting records. Do not create retrospective drafts or guarantee an appeal outcome.
